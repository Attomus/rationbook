# Changelog

## 0.1.2 — 29 October 2026

Attomus Labs release.

- Menu-bar capacity gauges, detailed views and local quota history for Codex and Claude Code.
- Manual Claude refresh or automatic updates every 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, with 15 minutes as the default.
- Last-known readings preserved during refresh failures, with freshness indicators and a Claude sign-in action.
- Essential and Full views anchored beneath the menu-bar icon, with scrolling for longer histories.
- Configurable reserves, optional capacity notifications, Open at Login and reversible Claude/CLI integrations.
- CLI access to versioned JSON status, history, diagnostics and conservative usage forecasts.
- Consistent current-reading status across Settings and the panel, with retained historical readings shown separately.
- Apache-2.0 licensing, a signed and notarised Apple Silicon macOS app, and Homebrew app and CLI packages.
